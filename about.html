Our Story

Why we started Tutoria

Tutoria was started by two people who care deeply about education: a research scholar from IISER Pune and a tech enthusiast who studied at Calcutta University. They saw a common problem — even in good private schools, the quality of teaching is often not enough for students to do their best. Many parents in cities like Kolkata and Siliguri face the same issue: finding good, reliable tutors who truly understand how their child learns.

Tutoria's goal is simple: connect students with the best tutors. All tutors are verified and chosen for their subject knowledge and teaching skills. Whether at home or online, every student gets personal attention and a learning plan that fits their needs.

Today, Tutoria works with families across metro cities, with a special focus on Kolkata and Siliguri in West Bengal. We know the pressure of school boards, exams, and fast-paced syllabi. Our tutoring is designed to support school studies while building clear concepts and confidence.

For parents in Kolkata, Siliguri, and other metros looking for home tutors or online tuition, Tutoria is a trusted partner in learning — helping every child study at their own pace, with the right tutor, in the right way.

What We Believe

The principles behind every match

01

Verification first

Every tutor is checked for identity, qualifications and subject knowledge before we ever recommend them to a family.

02

Understanding over speed

03

Built for West Bengal's boards

From WBBSE and WBCHSE to CBSE and ICSE, we look for tutors who already know the syllabus our students are facing.

No pressure, no lock-in

Free demo classes and free tutor replacements mean families never have to settle for a fit that isn't working.
